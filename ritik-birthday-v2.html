PERSONAL NOTE / PRIVATE EDITIONFOR RITIK, WITH RESPECT

Ritik, ek baat bolni thi.

Funny thing about online friendships — people sometimes assume they're somehow less real because they started on a screen. I don't see it that way. A screen can carry a conversation; it can't manufacture trust, comfort, humour, or the kind of understanding that makes a friendship matter.

Humari dosti ko koi fancy definition dene ki zaroorat nahi hai. Jo hai, jaisi hai, apni jagah special hai. I may not have a collection of photographs to put on this page, but that doesn't mean there's nothing worth remembering. Some things are better kept in the way they made you feel.

I hope you know that I genuinely appreciate you. Not because of one particular thing, or because you always have to be available, but simply because you're you. And I hope you give yourself the same patience and respect that you deserve from other people.

“Zindagi mein aage badhte rehna, apne dreams ko seriously lena, aur kabhi khud ko kam mat samajhna. Tere andar potential hai — bas kabhi-kabhi khud ko bhi yaad dilate rehna.”

For this new year of your life, I wish you progress you're proud of, peace that doesn't depend on everything going perfectly, good people who show up, and enough unexpected reasons to laugh on completely ordinary days.

And whenever life gets unnecessarily complicated, remember: there's a friend somewhere on this side of the screen who's rooting for you. No grand speech needed. Bas itna hi.

Happy Birthday, Ritik. Keep going. — Your friend
